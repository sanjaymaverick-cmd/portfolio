# Meridian

Personal advisory desk. It never sends an order.

## Language

**Review**:
A written look at the book that a person must accept, dismiss, or snooze.
_Avoid_: alert blast, trade idea, signal fire

**Daily PnL**:
The rupee effect of one quiet day, taken from net theta.
_Avoid_: carry, bleed, decay P&amp;L

**Gamma Scalping PnL**:
The extra rupee effect of a chosen market move from the ½ Γ (ΔS)² term. Long gamma can help. Short gamma can hurt.
_Avoid_: scalping profit, edge

**Not an order**:
Required words on every review. The desk never buys or sells.

**Vega action**:
One of six sized ways to move net vega. Each action also states the hit to delta, gamma, and theta.
_Avoid_: hedge instruction, must reduce
