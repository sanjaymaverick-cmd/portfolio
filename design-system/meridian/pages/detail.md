# Stock Detail / Deep Dive

A single name, researched. Two columns on ≥1280px.

**Left 58%**
- Identity row: symbol, name, exchange, sector, weight, recommendation badge
- Price chart (later) with SMA 20/50/200
- Dual-axis ρ and EWMA β
- Ownership / shareholding steps

**Right 42%**
- Factor radar (5 axes) + numeric column
- SHAP waterfall or signed bars
- Written recommendation (serif-adjacent sans, 13/1.55)
- Book lot: qty, avg, LTP, invested, MV, P&L
- Catalyst timeline

Recommendation badge is a hollow 1px pill, never a saturated fill.
